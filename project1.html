Project 1: How to Become a Physical Therapist

A personal career roadmap based on my major in Kinesiology and long-term goal of becoming a licensed Physical Therapist (PT).

Step 1: Build the Academic Foundation

Complete a bachelor's degree with strong preparation in anatomy, physiology, biology, psychology, and statistics. My major in Kinesiology gives me a strong base for movement science and rehabilitation concepts.

Step 2: Gain Observation Hours

Volunteer or work in clinics, hospitals, or outpatient rehabilitation settings to understand patient care, communication, and the daily responsibilities of physical therapists.

Step 3: Apply to DPT Programs

Apply to accredited Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) programs, prepare competitive applications, and highlight clinical experience, leadership, and academic readiness.

Step 4: Complete Clinical Training

During DPT study, complete clinical rotations to develop treatment planning, patient education, and evidence-based intervention skills across diverse populations.

Step 5: Pass Licensure Exams

After graduation, pass the required licensing exam and state requirements to begin practicing as a physical therapist.

Step 6: Continue Professional Growth

Build expertise through continuing education, mentorship, and optional specialty certifications in areas such as orthopedics, sports, pediatrics, or geriatrics.

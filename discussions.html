Overall Discussion Reflection

What Eight Artifacts Show About My Growth

These discussions and assignments show that learning became more meaningful when I connected course ideas to my own life. At the beginning of the course, I mainly focused on completing each task correctly. As I participated more often, I began to see discussion posts as a place to test my ideas, learn from classmates, and explain how class concepts apply beyond the classroom. Writing about my career goals made the steps toward physical therapy feel more organized, while the learning-strategy and time-management activities gave me tools I can use immediately.

The artifacts also show growth in communication and self-awareness. Responding to classmates required me to read carefully, respect perspectives that differed from mine, and write with a clear purpose. I became more aware that professional communication means listening as well as speaking. This is especially important for my future work in healthcare, where people need accurate information but also patience and encouragement.

Overall, the selected work reflects my effort to become a more intentional learner. I learned to plan ahead, use reliable information, and treat feedback as a resource instead of a setback. These are habits I will continue to use as I complete my Kinesiology education and prepare for the responsibilities of a physical therapy career.

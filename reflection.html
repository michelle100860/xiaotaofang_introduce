Overall Personal Reflection

A Year of Growth, Balance, and Direction

Reflecting on the habits, confidence, and purpose I developed through this course.

Completing this course has been an important part of my growth as a student and as a person preparing for a new career. I am balancing several roles: I am a mother, a sales manager, and a Kinesiology student with the long-term goal of becoming a physical therapist. At times, these responsibilities made it difficult to find enough time and energy for every assignment. However, the course reminded me that progress does not require perfection. It requires consistency, planning, and the willingness to keep learning when a task feels challenging.

One of my biggest lessons was learning how to connect academic work to my personal purpose. The interview project was especially meaningful because it gave me a more realistic view of physical therapy. I learned that a successful therapist needs more than scientific knowledge. They need to listen carefully, communicate with empathy, solve problems, and help patients feel hopeful during difficult moments. These ideas strengthened my commitment to this profession. They also helped me recognize that the communication skills I use in sales can become an asset in a future healthcare setting.

The discussion board activities helped me become a more active learner. Writing posts and reading classmates' perspectives pushed me to express my ideas more clearly and thoughtfully. I learned that I do not have to have every answer before joining a conversation. Asking a useful question, sharing an honest connection, and responding respectfully are all ways to learn. I also became more comfortable receiving feedback. Instead of seeing feedback as criticism, I now try to use it as information that helps me improve my next piece of work.

Time management was another major area of growth. Before this course, I often tried to manage everything in my head. That approach became stressful when work deadlines, family needs, and school assignments happened at the same time. I began using a weekly plan, breaking larger assignments into smaller steps, and setting aside specific study periods. These changes did not remove every challenge, but they made my responsibilities feel more manageable. I learned that being organized is not only about completing work on time; it is also a way to protect time for my family and for my own well-being.

Looking ahead, I will carry these lessons into my remaining coursework and career preparation. I plan to continue strengthening my foundation in anatomy, physiology, and movement science. I also want to seek observation opportunities that will help me understand different physical therapy settings and the needs of diverse patients. My goal is to enter a Doctor of Physical Therapy program with strong academic skills, professional habits, and a clear understanding of why this work matters to me.

This ePortfolio represents more than a collection of assignments. It documents a period when I became more confident in my ability to return to school, learn alongside other responsibilities, and move steadily toward a meaningful goal. I am proud of the effort I have made, and I know that the discipline, empathy, and curiosity I developed in this course will continue to guide me.
